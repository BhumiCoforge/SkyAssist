# SkyAssist Cancellation and Refund Policy

## Cancellation charges
- More than 72 hours before departure: INR 500 cancellation fee, the rest is refunded.
- Between 24 and 72 hours before departure: 50% of the fare is refunded.
- Less than 24 hours before departure: no refund.

## Refund timeline
Refunds go to the original payment method within 7 working days for cards and UPI, and within 14 working days for net banking.

## Airline-initiated cancellations
If SkyAssist cancels a flight, passengers can choose a full refund or a free rebooking on the next available flight.

## Name changes
Name corrections of up to 3 characters are free until 24 hours before departure. Full name changes are not allowed.
