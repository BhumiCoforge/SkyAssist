# SkyAssist Baggage Policy

## Cabin baggage
Each passenger may carry one cabin bag up to 7 kg with dimensions not exceeding 55 x 35 x 25 cm. One small personal item (handbag or laptop bag) is allowed in addition.

## Checked baggage
Domestic economy fares include 15 kg of checked baggage. Business fares include 25 kg. Extra weight is charged at INR 500 per kg if paid at the airport, or INR 350 per kg if pre-booked online at least 24 hours before departure.

## Restricted items
Power banks and lithium batteries must be in cabin baggage only. Flammable liquids, sharp objects and aerosols above 100 ml are not allowed in cabin baggage.

## Damaged or lost baggage
Report damaged or delayed baggage at the airline desk before leaving the airport. Written claims must be filed within 7 days for damage and 21 days for delayed baggage.
