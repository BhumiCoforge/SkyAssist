# SkyAssist Delay and Disruption Policy

## Delays
For delays over 2 hours, SkyAssist provides a meal voucher. For delays over 6 hours, passengers may choose a full refund or free rebooking.

## Overnight delays
If a delay requires an overnight stay, SkyAssist provides hotel accommodation and airport transfers.

## Missed connections
If a delay of a SkyAssist flight causes a missed connection on the same booking, SkyAssist rebooks the passenger on the next available flight at no cost.
