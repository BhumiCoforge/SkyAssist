# SkyAssist Check-in Policy

## Online check-in
Web check-in opens 48 hours before departure and closes 2 hours before departure.

## Airport check-in
Check-in counters open 3 hours before departure and close 60 minutes before departure for domestic flights. Baggage drop closes 45 minutes before departure.

## Boarding
Boarding gates close 25 minutes before departure. Passengers arriving after the gate closes may be denied boarding and treated as a no-show.

## Documents
Domestic passengers need a valid government photo ID such as Aadhaar, passport, driving licence or voter ID. Children under 2 years need a birth certificate.
